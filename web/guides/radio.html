guides / radio

      
weechat-radio:$ radio setup

      
Pick the picture that matches your station. Each path is about five minutes. All three work with the internet off.


      

        
You are the control operator

        
Stay inside your licence. Suggested frequencies are only suggestions — check your band plan before you transmit. See calling frequencies.

      


      

      
Four paths

      

        
          
D · VR-N76 / UV-PRO over Bluetooth

          
Radios with a built-in packet TNC. No cable, no sound card: the app pairs and connects for you.

          path D →
        
        
          
A · Handheld + Digirig

          
Walkie-talkie (Baofeng and friends) plus a USB Digirig or AIOC cable.

          path A →
        
        
          
B · Any radio with VOX

          
Headphone cable. The radio transmits when it hears data tones.

          path B →
        
        
          
C · HF rig with CAT

          
HF radio with USB. The computer can press PTT and change frequency.

          path C →
        
      


      
Audio: low / hot / good

      
The status bar reads the receive level from modem73 (the sound program sitting next to wcr).

      
    
        
  • low — turn radio volume up, or raise the computer input.
    • 
        
  • hot — turn it down. Distortion kills data.
    • 
        
  • good — leave it.
    • 
      


      
When the path is weak

      
If the other station is faint, or HF is fading, do not turn the audio up into hot. Pick a slower preset and let the program retry. Guide: weak signals.

      
hf-poor is the usual HF calling preset. hf-weak and hf-deep are slower. The status bar TX and RETRY fields show when the program has stepped down on its own.
