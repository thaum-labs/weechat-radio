weechat-radio:$ HF antennas

      
Which antenna to put up for HF, how long to cut it, and which bands it will work on. Pick a type in the calculator, slide the length and height, and it gives you the rest and a search term to build or buy one.


      

        
Antenna types

        
Orange dots are the current running along the antenna; green rings are the signal leaving it.

        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Calculator
    
        
    Lengths are for bare copper wire: cut a little long and trim. Bands in green work without a tuner, yellow ones work with a tuner or with less signal.
