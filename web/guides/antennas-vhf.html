weechat-radio:$ VHF and UHF antennas

      
On 2m and 70cm the antenna and its height decide how far you reach. Pick a type in the calculator, set the frequency and height, and it gives you the sizes, the range and a search term to build or buy one.


      

        
Antenna types

        
Orange dots are the current running along the antenna; green rings are the signal leaving it.
