guides / radio / vr-n76

      
weechat-radio:$ VR-N76 / UV-PRO over Bluetooth

      
No cable. The radio has its own packet TNC; the app talks to it over Bluetooth. About three minutes.


      

      

        
What you need

        
    
          
  • A Vero VR-N76, BTECH UV-PRO, Radioddity GA-5WB or VR-N7500, firmware 0.7.11+ (update in the HT app if the KISS menu is missing)
    • 
          
  • A computer with Bluetooth
    • 
          
  • WeeChat Radio 0.1.9 or newer
    • 
        

      


      
On the radio (once)

      
    
        
  1. Menu → General Settings → KISS TNC → Enable.
    2. 
        
  2. General Settings → Digital Mode → off. The radio’s own APRS logic otherwise fights the KISS stream.
    3. 
        
  3. Pick a simplex FM frequency for packet, low power. Leave the APRS frequency alone.
    4. 
        
  4. Menu → Pairing. Close the HT phone app — the radio takes one Bluetooth client at a time.
    5. 
      


      
In the app

      
    
        
  1. Choose VR-N76 / UV-PRO (Bluetooth) in setup (toolbar → setup if you are already configured).
    2. 
        
  2. Press Find radio. The app finds a paired radio or scans for ~8 s and pairs it for you (PIN 0000 is answered automatically).
    3. 
        
  3. Press Save and start. The station panel shows RADIO · VR-N76 linked in green and the radio shows its Bluetooth-data icon next to the power level.
    4. 
      

      
Terminal: wcr tnc find does the same. wcr tnc scan --inquiry lists what the computer sees. wcr tnc test --tx sends one identified test frame and prints anything heard.


      

        
How you know it worked

        
RADIO reads VR-N76 linked. Send a line in #bulletin: the radio’s TX light comes on for about two seconds. Other packet stations see you as M7TJF>WCR UI, a plain AX.25 frame with your callsign.

      


      
Quirks of the built-in TNC

      
    
        
  • One frame per PTT. The radio unkeys between frames, so the app paces them (frame_gap_ms). Long messages take a little longer than on a sound-card modem.
    • 
        
  • TXDELAY 600 ms. Lower clips the start of a frame; this is the default.
    • 
        
  • Keep frames short. Under ~200 bytes; the app fragments longer lines.
    • 
        
  • Linux: pair with bluetoothctl (pair + trust), then set tnc.bt_addr. If the desktop grabs the radio as a headset, add [General] Disable=Headset to /etc/bluetooth/input.conf.
    • 
        
  • macOS: use KISS TNC on a serial port with /dev/cu.VR-N76.
    • 
      


      
Other paths: handheld + Digirig · VOX cable · HF + CAT. Frequencies: calling.
