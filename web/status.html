weechat-radio:$ hub

      
The public meeting point on the internet. Stations that are not on the same radio still find each other here.

      
It lives at hub.weechatradio.com, on the same DigitalOcean droplet as this website. Caddy takes HTTPS on that machine and passes hub traffic to the wcr hub program. A station in Internet or Internet+Radio connects to wss://hub.weechatradio.com/ws. Radio and Radio+ keep working on the air if this machine is down. Radio+ still needs a gateway that can reach the hub when the message has to leave the radio.

      
What it holds is presence: callsign, mode, grid, and band, which is what the map draws. Chat text stays on the stations. Mail for callsign@mail.weechatradio.com also passes through here on its way to and from the internet. The figures below are read live from that machine. How the droplet is built is on deploy the hub.
