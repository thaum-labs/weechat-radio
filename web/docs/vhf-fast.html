weechat-radio:$ vhf-fast

      
The fastest VHF/UHF preset. Use it on a strong, clean local 2 m or 70 cm FM link, when there is no hiss on the signal.


      

        
What it is

        
Switch with /preset vhf-fast or /radio preset vhf-fast. The first mode is OFDM QAM16 at code rate 1/2: four bits in each symbol instead of two, with half the bits used for error correction. The modem picks a short frame when the data fits, so a chat line or a receipt keys in about 1 s instead of 2.6 s on vhf-fm.

        
If a receipt is missed, the next try steps down the ladder: QPSK 1/2, then RDM-1200S, then RDM-600S, and on down if needed. Near the edge of range it is no worse than vhf-fm. A station still set to vhf-fm can hear vhf-fast lines, as long as the signal is strong. Only the sender needs the preset.

        
It is for 2 m and 70 cm FM. Not for HF: QAM16 falls apart with fading.

      


      

        
How fast is it?

        
Speed is the modem's own rate after error correction. You get about a third to a half of it, because every frame takes its full time even when it is nearly empty, and every line waits for a receipt.

Those times are for a clear channel with the first try getting through. Retries and a busy channel add to it. A 1 KB mail is cut into pieces that fit the short frame, which is why it is about twice as fast as on vhf-fm. Big files on vhf-fast can use a normal QAM16 frame of up to 1 KB, about 2.6 s on the air.

        
Licensed VARA FM, on a radio with a 9600-baud or other flat-audio data port, is faster still on a strong FM path. How the two sit side by side: compare modes.

      


      

        
How strong the signal needs to be

        
9 dB is a textbook figure for QAM16 1/2 on a steady signal, not a lab measurement of this radio path. A simple check: if every line on vhf-fm gets [ok] first time and the received SNR stays at 10 dB or more, try vhf-fast. If lines start to need a second try, go back.

      


      

        
How to connect the radio

        
    
          
  • Cabled PTT is the most reliableA Digirig, a CM108 sound card, or CAT. There is no VOX lead, so the short 1 s frame is not clipped at the start.
    • 
          
  • VOX works on a strong linkOnce the level is set as in the VOX guide. An RT-900 at squelch 1, VOX 4 sent and took vhf-fast first time. If the start of frames gets clipped, use vhf-fm or a cabled PTT.
    • 
          
  • Untested over BluetoothBluetooth audio, for example a VR-N76, has not been tested on air with vhf-fast. It is offered there only when you turn on Experimental: allow vhf-fast over Bluetooth (desktop STATION panel, the Pi page's Dial, mode and preset box, or [modem] experimental_bt_vhf_fast = true in wcr.toml). The radio's audio compression (SBC) may break QAM16, and the modem then falls back to slower modes. A cable or VOX is the tested way.
    • 
        

      


      

        
Files and mail

        
    
          
  • Pictures in a direct chat are shrunk to fit 8 KB. Other small chat files stay at 4 KB.
    • 
          
  • On vhf-fast, a chat file goes in one burst. The other end says which pieces it missed, and only those go again. An 8 KB picture takes about 45 s.
    • 
          
  • Email can carry several files, up to 512 KB in all, over the radio on vhf-fast. On any other radio preset, files with mail are refused (a gateway sending over the internet can still take them on any preset).
    • 
        

      


      

        
Tips

        
    
          
  • Start on vhf-fm. Switch to vhf-fast only when the path is fully quiet.
    • 
          
  • Watch [ok] and the received SNR. A rising RETRY count, or a TX field that has stepped down to QPSK, means the link is not clean enough. Go back to vhf-fm.
    • 
          
  • The receiver already hears every preset. You can send vhf-fast while they stay on vhf-fm.
    • 
          
  • On a handheld, take short rests between long mail blocks so the radio can cool.
    •
