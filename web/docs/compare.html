What the columns mean

      

        

          
            
Trait51

          
          
            

              Weak SNR
              WSPR (~−31 dB) then FT8/JS8 (~−21 dB)
              AFSK 1200 / FM quieting required
            

            

              Fading
              Long coded symbols, interleave, or retries across a fade
              One dip prints garbage (PSK31, RTTY, APRS CRC)
            

            

              QRM
              Narrow or heavily coded; survives splatters
              No FEC; one hit corrupts the line
            

            

              Speed
              1200 baud packet or adaptive OFDM (VARA)
              WSPR 2 min beacon; FT8 15 s / FT4 7.5 s tiny payloads; JS8 / hf-deep chat
            

            

              Auto recover
              ACK + retries + mode step-down or ARQ (wcr HF, VARA)
              Beacon only (WSPR) or print-and-pray (PSK31, RTTY)
            

            

              Open
              Documented amateur mode (JS8, Olivia, PSK, RTTY, APRS)
              Closed (VARA). wcr envelope is open; modem73 is a separate modem
            

            

              Quiet VHF FM
              Handheld KISS / APRS on a clean FM channel
              HF-only waveforms
            

          
        

      


      
How to read the WeeChat Radio rows

      
hf-weak / hf-poor — mid SNR (3), strong fading recovery and retries (5), usable speed (3). Start on hf-poor. Drop to hf-weak when they are faint. This is the usual noisy-HF chat setting.

      
hf-deep — SNR 4, next to Olivia, below FT8/WSPR. Speed is 1. A short 1:1 line is about 2–5 seconds of PTT. The same line to #bulletin is about 10–15 seconds because HF groups send three Reed–Solomon pieces.

      
vhf-fm / handheld — same air as APRS (low SNR/fade/QRM). Auto recover is 4 because of ACKs and retries. Use it on a quiet FM channel, not as an HF weak-signal mode.

      
WSPR, FT8, and FT4 are weak-signal benchmarks. They do not carry a typed chat line.


      

        
What actually helps in noise

        
Helps a lot: coded waveforms (JS8, Olivia, MFSK-32R, RDM), retries across a fade, splitting a line so any 2 of 3 pieces rebuild it, waiting for a quiet channel.

        
Helps little: calling the payload “text” vs “binary.” CRC without FEC (APRS, AX.25, RTTY, PSK31) still loses the whole frame. A compact envelope only helps by spending less time on air.

      


      
Operator steps when the path is faint: weak signals. How internet vs radio is allowed: modes. Callsign email (not Winlink): email. Same text offline: wcr help compare.
