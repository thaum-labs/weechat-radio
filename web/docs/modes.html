weechat-radio:$ modes

      
Four ways to chat. Switch with /radio mode <name> or press F2 in the TUI.


      

      

        

          
internet

          
Radio off. You talk to the hub only. Fine for trying the program with no hardware.

        

        

          
internet-radio

          
Radio is primary. Internet fills gaps. This station is a gateway for others.

        

        

          
radio

          
Radio only. Frames are never put on the internet. Switching here asks you to confirm.

        

        

          
radio-plus

          
You stay off the internet. A nearby gateway that hears you may forward your traffic.

If the hub dies

      
      
If you are in internet-radio and the hub goes away, radio keeps working. The status bar says Internet down, radio only. Traffic that needed the internet waits in the hold queue and goes out when the hub returns.


      
Weak-signal behaviour

      
On a faint or fading path the program retries your message and asks modem73 for a slower waveform. The status bar shows TX (current waveform) and RETRY. Presets: hf-good, hf-poor, hf-weak, hf-deep.

      
Operator steps: weak signals. Packet layout: wire protocol.


      
Gateway knobs

      
In wcr.toml:

      
[gateway]
rf_egress = true          # put internet traffic on the air
third_party = "deny"      # guest (~nick) traffic on RF: allow or deny


      

        
How you know it worked

        
The status bar colour matches the cards above. Other stations on the map show the same mode marks.
