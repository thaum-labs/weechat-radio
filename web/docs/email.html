docs / email

      
weechat-radio:$ email

      
A second tab next to live chat, with the same station panel. In wcr gui from v0.1.74. Email does not use the live-chat transmit queue.


      

        
in the app

        
Run wcr update, then open wcr gui. You get an email tab next to live chat and an address at callsign@mail.weechatradio.com. See the changelog for the ship notes.

      


      
What it is for

      
Someone on the internet — a club secretary, an event organiser, anyone with normal email — can mail your callsign. You read and send that mail from the desktop app. On the hill you use radio. At home you use the hub. You do not create a Gmail account for WeeChat Radio, and you do not log the app into Gmail.

      
Your on-air identity stays your callsign. Your internet From address is callsign@mail.weechatradio.com. Replies stay on that address so they come back to the hub, then to you.


      
The Email tab

      
In wcr gui only (not the TUI or WeeChat). The station panel stays: mode, frequency, preset. The centre pane switches from live chat to email: inbox, outbox, sent, drafts, compose.

      
New mail puts a badge on email in the top bar. The tray icon can ping when you are not looking at Email. Guest names that start with ~ do not get an address; Email is hidden for them. In Setup, click live chat or email to leave setup. The logo does the same.


      
On the live map

      
Email shows as traffic, not a fifth operating mode. Your pin keeps its normal mode colour, with an e beside that same logo while mail is recent. The mode list uses the logo in orange plus that e. When mail went through another station's gateway, an orange arc links you and that gateway. The map ticker shows MAIL M7TJF -> G0ABC (callsigns only — never the internet address). Hub-only send still marks your pin when mail leaves or arrives.

      
Rewinding the map uses the mode and the lines from that time, not whatever the station is doing now. Pin colours for that rewind start once the hub has been recording them; lines come from the traffic already stored.


      
Your address

      
A licensed callsign is M7TJF@mail.weechatradio.com (your callsign). There is no domain box. Tell that address to people on the internet once.

      
Compose To: is an internet mailbox only, such as alex@example.com. Mail to another ham is still live chat. In Setup, Mail gateway is the internet-radio callsign on your dial. Radio+ without that callsign stays grey and the tooltip says to set it.


      
Send

      
      
Send never keys immediately. A confirm lists the destination, size, mode, frequency, preset, and a rough airtime. Internet and internet-radio say the message goes via the hub and does not use the radio. To, subject, and body stay on the form until the message is actually Sent.

      
Pure radio cannot reach the hub. The tab stays grey. The tooltip says to use Internet, Internet+Radio, or Radio+ with a gateway. Denying third-party chat relay does not stop a gateway from posting mail.

      
On HF and VOX the mail speed starts at the preset's robust rung and only gets slower. It does not move onto QPSK or OFDM. Chat is paused for the burst, then the modem is put back, including carrier sense.


      
When it says Sent

      
    
        
  • Hub (internet, internet-radio): Sent after the hub says Resend accepted the message.
    • 
        
  • VOX radio-plus: Sent after the gateway confirms that. Finishing the local transmit is still Outbox.
    • 
        
  • Other PTT (Digirig, CAT, a KISS TNC): Sent when this station's own burst has finished.
    • 
      

      
A toast timer does not mark Sent.


      
Receive — pull, not push

      
      
On radio-plus, Check mail asks the gateway for a short list (from, subject, size). You fetch a message and confirm the radio step. Nothing arrives on the radio until you ask.

Frequency and preset stay station-wide, same as chat. A 4 KB mail on hf-deep takes a long time on the air. The confirm exists so you see that before you key.


      
Optional copy to your own mailbox

      
Off by default. In Email settings you can ask the hub to also copy mail to a personal address (for example your usual inbox on a phone).

      
    
        
  • The hub emails a confirm code to that address. The toggle stays pending until you enter the code.
    • 
        
  • When it is on: mail you send can be BCC'd there; mail that arrives at the hub is copied there. Their Reply still waits at the hub. Copy-to does not steal the Reply off the radio path.
    • 
        
  • Do not auto-forward that mailbox back to callsign@mail.weechatradio.com or you will loop.
    • 
        
  • The personal address never goes over RF.
    • 
      


      
Limits

      
    
        
  • Plain text only, about 4 KB. No attachments.
    • 
        
  • Desktop GUI only.
    • 
        
  • Not a Gmail or Outlook client. The app never stores a mailbox password.
    • 
        
  • Not Winlink.
    • 
        
  • No encryption on the air. Mail on RF is as readable as chat. You are the control operator for third-party traffic to a non-ham.
    •

  3. They hit Reply. The hub stores it. The club radio stays quiet until someone asks.

  4. You tap Check mail, confirm, open the reply, confirm the airtime. The body comes over 144.950.

  5. If copy-to is on, step 3 also left a copy in your personal inbox. That copy does not use the radio.
