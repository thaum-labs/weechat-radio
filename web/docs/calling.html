weechat-radio:$ calling frequencies

      
Places other WeeChat Radio stations may listen. Mode is the radio setting: USB or LSB on HF, FM or AM elsewhere. Suggestions, not assignments. WW = worldwide.


      

        
Check your band plan

        
You are the control operator. Verify the frequency is legal for your licence and country before you transmit. The setup wizard only sets the radio via rigctl if you confirm. Do not use APRS channels (144.800 UK/EU, 144.390 US) for chat.

        
HF data is USB, including 160/80/40 (voice on those bands is LSB). 60m is USB. CB is listed per region: AM/FM for typical sets, USB/LSB for SSB-capable radios. Channel numbers are not the same in every country.
