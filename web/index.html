weechat-radio:$ ./about▌

Decentralised chat for Radio Operators.

weechat-radio:$ help modes

Optional Hybrid Messaging.

You’re out with a radio and no internet. Your friend is at home on their laptop in another country. Here’s what happens when you send them a message.

  1. You send it

    It goes out over the radio. Your end never touches the internet.

  2. Someone nearby hears it

    A station on the same frequency that’s also online picks it up.

  3. It goes online

    They pass it on over the internet, and your message shows [gw].

  4. Your friend gets it

    It pops up on their laptop, wherever they are.

  5. You know it arrived

    A receipt comes back the same way, and your message turns [ok].

weechat-radio:$ /radio mode

Radio to radio, or via a hybrid connection.

Switch whenever you like. Your icon and your pin on the map change colour, so everyone can see how you’re connected.

internet

No radio? No problem

Chat over the internet. Good for friends, family, or anyone without a licence.

internet-radio

Radio and internet together

You send over the air and pass nearby stations’ messages online. You’re the link between the two.

radio

Radio only

Your messages go out over the air and stay there, even if a station with internet hears you.

radio-plus

Radio, with a helping hand

Only a radio here, but a station with internet that hears you can pass your message on.

weechat-radio:$ help hops

Built-in mesh network features.

When a station hears your message, it keeps a copy and sends it on. By day on 40m, London is too far from Kyiv for a clean signal, but Frankfurt and Kraków each hear their neighbours clearly and pass it along. The receipt comes back the same way. One frequency, one chat room, chat with everyone without the internet.

A direct message can be passed on three times. Start it with ! and it goes a step further, or !! in an emergency for the furthest reach. More on hops.
