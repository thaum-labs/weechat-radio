v1.0.18-rc5 is a release candidate. Get it with `wcr update --rc`.

## What changed since v1.0.18-rc4

**Range test: receipts that come back late now count (#342)**
- On a 15 Nov drive test, the home station answered 63 probes, but each answer came back 25 s to 2.5 min late, so the map showed no green. A probe now waits up to 3 minutes for its own receipt. A late receipt still turns that probe green ("both ways").
- A new probe goes out only once the last one has left for the radio. On a busy or noisy channel, probes used to pile up in the send queue. That made receipts late and held up other traffic, such as the mail gateway ask.
- `/rangetest off` now also cancels a probe still waiting to be sent, so nothing more goes out after you stop.
