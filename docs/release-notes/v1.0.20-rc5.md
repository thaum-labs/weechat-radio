v1.0.20-rc5 is a release candidate. Get it with `wcr update --rc`.

## What changed since v1.0.20-rc4 (QA #365)

Tobias picked new marker layouts from mockups (layouts C and B).

**Desktop app: short progress beside the marks**

- A long message's progress is now short and sits just left of its marks: `■□ 1/2 [tx] [x1]` while sending, `✓✓ 2/2 [ok]` when delivered, `⋯ 2/3 [rf]` while arriving, `✓ 3/3 [rf]` when complete, `⚠ 2/3` when a piece is missing or undelivered.
- Hover over it for the full words ("sending 1/2 … awaiting receipt", "✓ complete · 3/3", …).

**Pi web app: marks and progress on their own row**

- For a long message, the marks and the progress go on one right-aligned row under the text, for example `[rf] ✓ complete · 3/3` or `[tx] sending 1/2 ■□ … awaiting receipt`. It looks the same on a phone. Ordinary lines keep their marks inline.

Everything else is as in rc4.
