v1.0.20-rc7 is a release candidate. Get it with `wcr update --rc`. It carries everything from rc6.

## New preset: vhf-fast

For strong local 2 m and 70 cm FM links, where the signal is fully quieted (no hiss).

- Pick it with `/preset vhf-fast`, or from the preset picker in the desktop app or on the Pi page.
- It sends OFDM QAM16 1/2, and modem73 picks the frame size for each packet. Chat lines and receipts go in short frames of about 1 s, instead of 2.6 s on `vhf-fm`. Mail is only about 10% faster than on `vhf-fm` in rc7 (fixed in rc8).
- A missed receipt drops back to QPSK 1/2 (the same as `vhf-fm`), then down the usual ladder. Near the edge of range it is no worse than `vhf-fm`.
- Stations on `vhf-fm` hear `vhf-fast` lines, and the other way round. Both sides need a strong signal to decode QAM16.
- Small files (`/file`) work on `vhf-fast` too.
- It is not offered on Bluetooth radios, such as the VR-N76 over Bluetooth audio. The radio's audio codec already breaks 8PSK (`hf-good`), and QAM16 is denser. Use a cabled radio (Digirig, CM108, sound card).
- `vhf-fm` and every HF preset behave exactly as before.

## Mail airtime estimate

- The `~N s on <preset>` hint in the mail composer now uses modem73's real frame times. Before, it was about 40% too low on `vhf-fm`.

## What to test

1. Two cabled stations close together, both on `vhf-fast`: send chat both ways and a mail. Lines should show `[ok]` in about half the time they take on `vhf-fm`.
2. Move further apart until lines need a retry. The TX field should show `QPSK 1/2` on the retry, and the line should still arrive.
3. One station on `vhf-fast` and one on `vhf-fm`: chat should work both ways.
