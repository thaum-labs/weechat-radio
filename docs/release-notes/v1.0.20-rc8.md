v1.0.20-rc8 is a release candidate. Get it with `wcr update --rc`. It carries everything from rc7 and rc6.

## vhf-fast: mail about twice as fast

- In rc7, mail on `vhf-fast` was cut into 300-byte pieces. With headers added, each piece was just too big for modem73's 1 s short frame, so it went out as a 2.6 s frame. A 1 KB mail took about 15 s, against 17 s on `vhf-fm`.
- On `vhf-fast`, mail pieces now fit the short frame (254 bytes), so a 1 KB mail takes about 9 s.
- This applies to sending, gateway replies and fetched mail on a `vhf-fast` station. Every other preset cuts mail exactly as before.
- A station on any version reassembles the smaller pieces.

## Website

- The Weak signals page has a new section, "How fast is it?". It gives each preset's speed next to the dial-up modem it matches, with times for a chat line and a 1 KB mail, and explains what `vhf-fast` needs (real PTT, not Bluetooth or VOX).

## What to test

1. Send a mail of about 1 KB from a `vhf-fast` station (the Pi with the Digirig) through a gateway. The composer hint should say about 9 s, and the mail should arrive whole.
2. Do the same on `vhf-fm`: about 17 s, and unchanged from rc6.
