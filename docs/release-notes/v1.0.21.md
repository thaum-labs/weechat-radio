v1.0.21 gives email a cleaner screen in the desktop app and on the Pi page. Run `wcr update` to get it. On an RC, press Update on the web page or in the desktop app.

## What changed since v1.0.20

**Email: one thing at a time**
- The desktop app and the Pi page now show the folder list, an opened mail, or the compose form, one at a time. Compose opens with **+ New** (Pi: **New mail**) or **Reply**, not under every list.
- **Reply** is now in the desktop app too: it fills To and `Re: subject`.
- **Delete** sits on the opened mail. The Pi page can now delete mail (it could not before). A waiting header keeps its own delete on the list line.
- The list marks mail not read yet with a dot and mail with files with 📎. Received mail no longer carries a "Received" chip; chips show only when they say something (Waiting, On air, Failed, Delivered…).
- Desktop: the empty Drafts tab is gone, Copy-to moved under the **⚙** button, times are short and local ("07:37", "Thu", "10 Nov", "Today 07:37"), and a sent mail's journey is one line ("08:07 written → 08:12 burst done… → 08:13 accepted by Resend").

Nothing on the air changed, so v1.0.21 talks to v1.0.20 and earlier exactly as before.
