v1.0.15 is the final release of the v1.0.15 series. It is the same code as v1.0.15-rc8, which passed QA (#266). Run `wcr update` to get it.

## What changed since v1.0.14 (rc1 to rc8)

**Fewer collisions and safer receipts**
- On FM, stations waiting to send after the channel goes quiet now start at least 1.3 seconds apart, so the later one hears the first and waits (rc1).
- Receipts wait until your own message or beacon has gone out, then check the channel is free before they send. They no longer slip out behind your own message while the modem is still waiting to send it, and they keep waiting while another station's long message is on the air, even one your modem could not decode (rc2 to rc5).
- Your own messages and beacons wait until a pending receipt has actually gone out, and a receipt waits a random moment (up to 2 s) before checking the channel (rc4).

**Bundled modem**
- modem73 now prepares a message's audio before its busy test, so the radio keys as soon as the channel is clear. On a Raspberry Pi 3 that preparation took about a second, long enough for another station to start first. Linux, Pi and macOS; Windows unchanged (rc6).

**Pi web page and gateway view**
- Restarting or updating the web service no longer puts the station on the air by itself; it comes back only if you left it running (rc2).
- Gateway view shows Pi health; the spectrum keeps its last good reading for up to 2 s instead of flickering to "no audio" (rc7).
- Design pass to match the website: header, wordmark font, tab icon, slim scrollbar, no errors while stopped, Stop then Start clears the old note (rc2 to rc5).
- "On the air": the right-hand rf column no longer hides under overlay scrollbars on Macs and phones, and mail frames name their sender and receiver instead of "?" (rc8).

**Other**
- A Radio+ station no longer sends a bulletin back on air when the only station around is the one that sent it; TX level is always the one in your settings; Bluetooth clipping hint (rc2).
- chain_timed bulletin test waits until the stations have heard each other, so it stops failing at random (rc7).

## Known issues / backlog

Tracked in #271 (v1.0.16 backlog):
- Random retry wait after a collision.
- Move to stock modem73 v2.5.0.
- Occasional on-air overlaps.
- Pi RX clipping just after its own TX.
- Pi missing frames over Bluetooth.
- Empty spectrum after login (about 1 in 60).
- macOS mail_harness start-up timing flake (`a_long_mail_at_real_airtime_is_accepted_on_the_first_pass`).
