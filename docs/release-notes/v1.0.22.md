v1.0.22 adds an experimental setting to try `vhf-fast` over Bluetooth audio (QA #392). Run `wcr update` to get it.

## What changed since v1.0.21

**Experimental: allow vhf-fast over Bluetooth (off by default)**
- New setting `[modem] experimental_bt_vhf_fast` in `wcr.toml`. Desktop app: the **BT FAST** row in the STATION panel. Pi page: **Experimental: allow vhf-fast over Bluetooth** in the *Dial, mode and preset* box. In chat: `/btfast on` or `/btfast off`.
- With it on, a Bluetooth audio radio (VR-N76, UV-PRO, GA-5WB…) is offered `vhf-fast`, tagged **experimental**, with a warning: the radio's Bluetooth audio compression (SBC) may break QAM16, and the modem then falls back to slower modes. A saved `vhf-fast` now survives a restart while the setting is on.
- `hf-good` stays refused on Bluetooth audio either way. Cabled, Digirig and VOX radios are not affected.
- While a Bluetooth radio runs `vhf-fast` on this setting, `/status` ends with `experimental: vhf-fast over Bluetooth` and the station log says so at start.
- Turning the setting off on a Bluetooth radio that sits on `vhf-fast` moves it back to `vhf-fm`, exactly as before.
- The desktop app now shows a preset picker for Bluetooth audio radios too (before, the preset was read-only there). It lists only the presets the link allows.

**Untested over Bluetooth** until QA posts an on-air result.

Nothing on the air changed, so v1.0.22 talks to v1.0.21 and earlier exactly as before.
