v1.0.16-rc1 is a release candidate. Get it with `wcr update --rc`.

## What changed since v1.0.15

**Mail gateway setting (#274)**
- The "Mail gateway" setting in setup shows only in Radio+ mode, in the desktop app and on the Pi web page, with one line explaining it: "Callsign of the internet-radio station that posts your email, e.g. your home Pi."
- It has two choices: Auto (the default) or a typed callsign. Changing mode keeps whatever you saved.
- In Radio+ with no gateway and Auto off, the mail tab says why mail cannot go instead of greying out.

**Mail gateway Auto (#275)**
- Auto asks over the air which internet-radio stations hear you. Each gateway that hears the signed ask reports its signal (SNR) and position to the hub, and the hub gives the best three a reply slot each, best signal first, never overlapping.
- Each reply carries the signal the gateway heard; your station measures the reply's signal too. The pick is the gateway with the best weaker-of-the-two signal; distance only breaks a tie.
- The pick shows in setup and status ("Auto: TF101, link 6 dB, 1 more heard"). If nobody answers: "No mail gateway heard".
- Your station asks again at start, after a band or preset change, after about an hour, and when the picked gateway stops answering. Mail falls back to the next gateway heard.
- Slot timings follow the preset's airtime on every band.
- Mail and Check mail go to the one picked gateway. The hub also drops a second post of the same mail id, as a safety net.
- The outbox row and mail timeline show the gateway actually used ("via TF101").
- While a gateway is being found, the send bar shows "Finding a mail gateway: Not sent yet" before it moves on to sending.
- A typed callsign still pins one gateway, and older settings with a gateway typed in stay pinned.
