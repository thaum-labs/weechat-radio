v1.0.20 is the final release of the v1.0.20 series: everything from v1.0.20-rc1 to rc14, with no changes since rc14. Run `wcr update` to get it. On an RC, press Update on the web page or in the desktop app.

## What changed since v1.0.19 (rc1 to rc14)

**New preset: vhf-fast (rc7, rc8)**
- For strong local 2 m and 70 cm FM links on a cabled radio (Digirig, CM108, sound card). OFDM QAM16 1/2 with modem73 picking the frame size: chat lines and receipts in about 1 s frames, a 1 KB mail in about 9 s (17 s on `vhf-fm`).
- A missed receipt drops back to QPSK 1/2, then down the usual ladder. `vhf-fm` and `vhf-fast` stations hear each other. Not offered on Bluetooth radios.

**Files and pictures (rc9 to rc14)**
- Pictures in direct chats on `vhf-fast`: anything over 8 KB is shrunk to fit (JPEG, no metadata). Other small files stay at 4 KB.
- Chat files on `vhf-fast` go in one burst; the far end reports missing pieces once and only those go again. An 8 KB picture takes about 45 s. Setting **File send**: auto, burst or pieces.
- Email carries files: several per mail, up to 512 KB in all on `vhf-fast` over the radio, or on any preset for a gateway sending over the internet. Big mail goes in blocks with progress and Cancel. Files emailed to you come over the radio up to 8 KB.
- Setting **Radio type** (Handheld, Mobile, Base) rests the radio between long mail blocks so it does not overheat.
- The bundled modem73 has a new `keyed-bursts` patch: a whole file burst or mail block goes out in one transmission, VOX included. Cancel also clears frames modem73 already holds.
- Gateway reports go on QPSK on `vhf-fast`, and progress puts the sender back on the first rung, so lost reports no longer slow resends.

**Chat (rc3 to rc6)**
- Long messages go in `[1/3]` pieces and show as one entry that fills in, with short progress beside the marks (`■□ 1/2 [tx]`, `3/3 [rf]`, `⚠ 2/3`).
- Lines are never split inside an emoji. The desktop app shows emoji as names (`:+1:`) and sends the real emoji.
- Desktop input grows up to 5 lines; chat text can be selected and copied. Other stations' callsigns are cyan.

**Desktop app and Pi page (rc1 to rc4)**
- Windows: the IN meter now moves while the station only listens (#364).
- modem73 layout: MAIL GW key in the ACTIVITY panel (#362), HEARD list in STATION, SNR LOG in SIGNAL.
- Changing the preset restarts modem73 by itself once the radio is idle, everywhere, and `/status` shows the preset really running.

**VOX setup and help (#365)**
- Setup and guides say squelch 1 and a mid VOX level (4 on a VR-N76), with the trade-off stated plainly.

The radio signal for every existing preset is unchanged, so v1.0.20 talks to v1.0.19 and v1.0.18. Bigger files, bursts and several attachments need v1.0.20 at both ends (and on the gateway).
