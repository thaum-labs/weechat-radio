WeeChat Radio — suggested data/packet calling presets
Verify your band plan before you transmit. You are the control operator.

Mode is the radio setting: USB or LSB on HF, FM or AM elsewhere.
HF data is USB, including 160/80/40 (voice on those bands is LSB). 60m is USB.
CB is listed per region. AM/FM for typical sets; USB/LSB for SSB-capable radios.
WW = worldwide.
